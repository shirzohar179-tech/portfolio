01 / Psychology → Design

What psychology taught me about design.

Before I opened Figma, I spent years studying why people notice, forget, hesitate and act. That's the lens I bring to every screen I design.

  • 01

    Cognitive load

    From cognitive psychology

    Working memory can only hold a handful of things at once. I design screens that remember for people, so they don't have to.

  • 02

    Mental models

    From perception & learning

    People arrive with expectations shaped by every other product they use. I research those first, then design with them, not against them.

  • 03

    Motivation & habit

    From behavioral psychology

    Behavior happens when motivation, ability and a prompt meet. I make the right action the easy one, and put it at the right moment.
